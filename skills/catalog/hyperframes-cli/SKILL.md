# Check and render local HyperFrames compositions

Use the installed CLI with HYPERFRAMES_NO_TELEMETRY=1. Discover version-specific options with its help. Validate the composition, inspect frames for legibility and cropping, then render MP4 into the chosen workspace. Verify duration, aspect ratio, audio and captions.
